# Free 7-Day Competitor Revenue Sprint

A lightweight operating system for founders, agencies and small-business operators who want to turn competitor changes into revenue actions instead of passive research.

## Day 1 — Baseline
Record each competitor's core offer, entry price, premium price, primary CTA, guarantee, trial/demo mechanism and lead capture.

## Day 2 — Pricing
Check plan names, price points, bundles, discounts, annual incentives, setup fees and hidden friction. Write one pricing hypothesis worth testing.

## Day 3 — Positioning
Capture the headline, target customer, promised outcome, proof mechanism and strongest differentiation claim. Rewrite your own offer in one sentence with a sharper outcome.

## Day 4 — Acquisition
Review current landing pages, SEO pages, lead magnets, public tools, partnerships, directories and referral mechanics. Choose one channel you can launch without paid spend.

## Day 5 — Conversion
Compare CTA visibility, checkout steps, trust signals, urgency, guarantees, FAQs and objection handling. Remove one avoidable conversion step from your own funnel.

## Day 6 — Promotion
Check limited-time offers, seasonal campaigns, bundles, referral incentives, free trials and content pushes. Select one legitimate promotion to test.

## Day 7 — Revenue decision
Rank observed opportunities by:

`probability of sale × gross margin × ticket size × scalability ÷ time-to-launch`

Execute the highest-scoring action first. Keep the rest in a backlog and repeat the sprint weekly.
