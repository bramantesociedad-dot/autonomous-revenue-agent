# Hotel & Tourism Scavenger Hunt Revenue Guide

A practical framework for hotels, resorts, tourism operators, events and venues that want to turn a location into a branded guest experience.

## What a branded mission can do

A well-designed scavenger hunt or digital mission can create:

- a new paid guest activity;
- an upsell bundled with rooms, tours or events;
- family and couple experiences during low-demand hours;
- team-building packages for corporate groups;
- sponsor placements and local partner offers;
- shareable content that supports organic discovery.

## Basic experience architecture

1. **Narrative:** Give participants a simple story, objective and role.
2. **Route:** Use safe, accessible locations that support the story.
3. **Challenges:** Mix observation, riddles, photo tasks and local discovery.
4. **Progress:** Make each completed step unlock the next one.
5. **Finale:** End at a memorable attraction, venue, reward or partner location.
6. **Monetization:** Decide whether the mission is sold standalone, bundled, sponsored or used to increase spend elsewhere.

## Commercial models to test

- **Per guest:** charge per participant or family group.
- **Room upsell:** add the experience during booking/check-in.
- **Corporate package:** fixed fee for a private team experience.
- **Sponsored route:** local businesses fund inclusion or rewards.
- **White-label:** the venue owns a branded version and promotes it as its own experience.
