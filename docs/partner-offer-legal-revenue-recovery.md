# Legal Intake Revenue Recovery — Partner Offer

## What it solves
Law firms often have paid acquisition value trapped in missed calls, unbooked consultations, stale intake leads and incomplete follow-up. This offer is designed for agencies and RevOps/intake partners that already serve law firms and want a measurable post-acquisition revenue product.

## 30-day pilot
1. Quantify the historical backlog using aggregate, non-sensitive pipeline counts.
2. Segment missed/stale enquiries by source, age, matter category and commercial value where authorized.
3. Define reactivation and follow-up workflows inside the firm's existing intake/CRM stack.
4. Track re-opened enquiries, booked consultations, qualified matters and attributable collected revenue where available.
5. Produce a partner/client results report and rollout recommendation.

## Commercial model
- Initial pilot: USD 2,500 for one firm when the backlog economics justify it.
- Pilot fee may be credited toward a longer rollout.
- Agency/partner retains the client relationship and may package or mark up the service.
- Portfolio and recurring pricing is set after the pilot based on account count, recoverable value and implementation scope.

## Qualification
Best fit: firms with meaningful inbound lead volume, paid acquisition, an established intake/CRM process, and a historical pool of missed or dormant enquiries.

## Compliance boundary
Start with aggregate non-sensitive data. Access to client or matter-level information requires appropriate authorization and must remain within the firm's compliant systems and applicable professional/privacy rules.

## Measurement
The service does not promise a fixed recovery amount. Success is measured from verified downstream outcomes: consultations booked, qualified matters reopened, collected revenue attributable to the recovery workflow, and cost-to-recovery.
