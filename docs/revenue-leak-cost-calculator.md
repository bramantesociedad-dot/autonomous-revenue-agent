# Free Revenue Leak Cost Calculator

Estimate how much monthly revenue a website may be leaving on the table before paying for more traffic.

## 1. Enter your current numbers

- Monthly website visitors: **V**
- Current conversion rate: **C** (as a decimal; 2% = 0.02)
- Average order / customer value: **A**
- Gross margin: **M** (as a decimal)

Current monthly revenue:

`V × C × A`

Current monthly gross profit:

`V × C × A × M`

## 2. Model small conversion improvements

A useful first test is not an unrealistic doubling. Model +10%, +25% and +50% relative improvement in conversion rate.

Example: if your current conversion rate is 2%, a +25% relative improvement means 2.5%, not 27%.

Projected monthly revenue at improved conversion rate **C2**:

`V × C2 × A`

Incremental monthly revenue:

`V × (C2 - C) × A`

Incremental monthly gross profit:

`V × (C2 - C) × A × M`

## Example

A site with 10,000 monthly visitors, a 2% conversion rate and a $120 average customer value produces:

`10,000 × 0.02 × $120 = $24,000/month`

If conversion improves from 2% to 2.5%:

`10,000 × 0.025 × $120 = $30,000/month`

That is **$6,000 additional monthly revenue** from the same traffic.
