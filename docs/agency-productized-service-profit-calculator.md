# Agency Productized Revenue Service Profit Calculator

Use this simple model to decide whether adding a white-label revenue-intelligence service can make economic sense for your agency before you build anything internally.

## Inputs

Choose realistic values for your agency:

- **Active clients:** number of clients you could reasonably offer the service to.
- **Adoption rate:** percentage of those clients likely to buy.
- **Monthly client price:** what you would charge per participating client.
- **Monthly delivery cost:** internal or outsourced delivery cost per participating client.
- **Setup / onboarding fee:** optional one-time fee charged to each participating client.

## Core formulas

**Participating clients** = Active clients × Adoption rate

**Monthly recurring revenue** = Participating clients × Monthly client price

**Monthly gross profit** = Participating clients × (Monthly client price − Monthly delivery cost)

**One-time setup revenue** = Participating clients × Setup fee

**Annualized gross profit** = Monthly gross profit × 12

## Example

An agency with 20 active clients offers a recurring revenue-intelligence service to 25% of them.

- Active clients: 20
- Adoption: 25%
- Participating clients: 5
- Client price: $249/month
- Delivery cost: $79/month
- Setup fee: $300

Result:

- Monthly recurring revenue: **$1,245**
- Monthly gross profit: **$850**
- Annualized gross profit: **$10,200**
- One-time setup revenue: **$1,500**

The purpose is not to promise a result. It is to test whether the unit economics are attractive enough to justify a real client pilot.
