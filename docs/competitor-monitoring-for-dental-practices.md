# Competitor Monitoring for Dental Practices

Dental practices compete on more than price. New-patient offers, financing, implants, whitening, emergency availability, reviews, guarantees, consultation language and booking friction can change demand quickly.

## Weekly competitor revenue checklist

Track 3–5 local competitors and record:

- headline offer and primary CTA;
- implant, cosmetic, emergency and hygiene promotions;
- financing language and payment plans;
- consultation pricing or free-consult offers;
- booking friction and response options;
- review count, proof and before/after positioning;
- new landing pages, paid-offer pages and seasonal promotions;
- content themes that repeatedly attract commercial intent.

Do not copy competitors. Use the changes as signals to improve your own positioning, offer architecture and conversion path.

## What to act on first

Prioritize changes that are specific, measurable and close to revenue: clearer treatment-specific landing pages, faster booking, stronger proof, transparent next steps, differentiated financing and high-intent service pages.
