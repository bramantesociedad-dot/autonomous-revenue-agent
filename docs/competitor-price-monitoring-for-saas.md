# Competitor Price Monitoring for SaaS

SaaS pricing changes quickly: plan names, monthly and annual rates, free-trial terms, usage limits, seat pricing, add-ons, bundles and promotional offers can all shift without notice.

A simple weekly competitor-pricing review helps revenue teams spot changes before they become obvious in win/loss data.

## What to monitor every week

Track each important competitor's:

- entry, mid-tier and premium pricing;
- monthly vs. annual discount;
- free-trial or freemium terms;
- usage, seat and feature limits;
- new bundles and add-ons;
- positioning and headline changes;
- signup and checkout friction;
- promotions, discounts and guarantees;
- newly emphasized customer segments;
- major content or landing-page changes.

## Turn observations into revenue experiments

A useful monitoring system does not stop at screenshots. Each meaningful change should generate a commercial hypothesis: adjust packaging, test a stronger value metric, clarify differentiation, reposition a plan, add a comparison page, change a trial, or test a new offer.
