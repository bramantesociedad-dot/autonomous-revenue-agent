# Competitor Monitoring for Med Spas

Med spas compete through bundles, financing, first-visit offers, memberships, treatment positioning, social proof and booking speed. Small changes in packaging can materially shift conversion.

## Weekly competitor revenue checklist

Monitor 3–5 competitors for:

- first-visit and consultation offers;
- membership pricing and recurring packages;
- treatment bundles and seasonal promotions;
- financing and payment-plan messaging;
- before/after proof and review positioning;
- booking flow and response speed;
- treatment-specific landing pages;
- new content themes tied to high-intent services.

The goal is not imitation. The goal is to detect market movement early and turn it into differentiated offers and better conversion.

## Highest-value opportunities

Look first for recurring memberships, treatment bundles, abandoned high-intent pages, unclear pricing, weak proof, slow booking and generic service descriptions.
