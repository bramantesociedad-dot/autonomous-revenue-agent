# Competitor Revenue Monitoring Checklist

A lightweight weekly competitor-analysis framework for small businesses that want to spot pricing, offer and positioning changes before they become obvious.

## Check these 8 signals every week

1. **Headline / positioning:** Did the competitor change who they target or the outcome they promise?
2. **Pricing:** Did price, packaging, discounts or payment terms change?
3. **Offer structure:** New bundles, guarantees, bonuses, trials, subscriptions or upsells?
4. **Calls to action:** Are they pushing demo, buy-now, WhatsApp, booking, quote or free trial more aggressively?
5. **Proof:** New testimonials, case studies, certifications, media mentions or client logos?
6. **Content:** Which topics are being published repeatedly and appear tied to buyer intent?
7. **Promotions:** Any urgency, seasonal campaigns, coupons or limited-time offers?
8. **Distribution:** New marketplaces, partnerships, affiliates, social channels or local landing pages?

## Turn monitoring into revenue decisions

For each meaningful competitor change, answer three questions:

- What buyer problem are they trying to capture?
- Does the change make their offer easier or harder to buy than ours?
- What is one measurable response we can test this week?

Do not imitate blindly. The goal is to identify **market signals** and create a better response for your own customers.

## Weekly action template

Record:

- competitor;
- observed change;
- likely commercial intent;
- threat/opportunity level;
- recommended response;
- test owner;
- deadline;
- result next week.
