# Ecommerce Conversion Audit Checklist

Traffic is expensive even when it is organic. Before chasing more visitors, check whether the store is leaking revenue at the points where buyers decide, hesitate or abandon.

## Product page

- Can a first-time buyer understand the product and outcome in seconds?
- Is the primary call to action obvious on mobile?
- Are price, shipping expectations and returns easy to find?
- Is there credible proof such as reviews, customer photos, guarantees or demonstrated results?
- Are the most common objections answered before checkout?

## Offer

- Is there a clear reason to buy now rather than later?
- Are bundles, quantity breaks or complementary products used where they improve order value?
- Does the offer emphasize the buyer outcome rather than only product features?
- Is the difference versus alternatives clear?

## Cart and checkout

- Are surprise costs minimized?
- Is guest checkout available where appropriate?
- Is the mobile flow short and readable?
- Are payment options clear before the final step?
- Are abandoned-cart and checkout-start events measured?

## Revenue measurement

Track product-page view → add-to-cart → checkout-start → paid-order conversion. The largest drop usually tells you where to investigate first.
