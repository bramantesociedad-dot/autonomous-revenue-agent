Hotel marketing, revenue-management and direct-booking agencies already own the client relationship. The missing product is often a lightweight recurring intelligence layer that shows clients what competitors changed and what revenue action to take next.

The job-to-be-done is not “more data.” It is giving hotel clients a recurring, concrete answer to three questions: what changed in the market, why it matters to revenue, and what should we test next.
