# Hotel Direct-Booking Revenue Leak Calculator

A simple way for independent hotels, boutique properties and hospitality agencies to estimate how much direct-booking revenue may be lost to weak website conversion before buying more traffic.

## 1. Enter your current numbers

- Monthly website visitors: `V`
- Current direct-booking conversion rate: `C`
- Average booking value: `A`
- Gross margin on a direct booking: `M`

Use conversion and margin as decimals. Example: 1.5% = `0.015`; 70% margin = `0.70`.

## 2. Current monthly direct-booking revenue

`Current revenue = V × C × A`

Example:

- 10,000 monthly visitors
- 1.5% conversion
- $600 average booking value

Current direct-booking revenue = `10,000 × 0.015 × $600 = $90,000/month`.

## 3. Estimate the upside from a small conversion improvement

Choose a realistic target conversion rate `T`.

`Incremental revenue = V × (T - C) × A`

If the same property improves from 1.5% to 1.8%:

`10,000 × (0.018 - 0.015) × $600 = $18,000 additional monthly direct-booking revenue`.

Estimated incremental gross profit at 70% margin:

`$18,000 × 0.70 = $12,600/month`.

## 4. Where hotel websites commonly leak revenue

Check these before spending more on acquisition:

- unclear room/package value proposition above the fold;
- weak or hidden Book Direct CTA;
- rate/benefit parity not explained;
- no compelling direct-booking advantage;
- generic room copy that does not answer buying objections;
- lack of trust signals near the booking decision;
- mobile friction between landing page and booking engine;
- packages and experiences buried in navigation;
- weak urgency or availability framing;
- no useful upsell path for experiences, transfers, dining or local activities.
