# Competitor Monitoring for Boutique Hotels

Boutique hotels compete through room packaging, direct-booking perks, minimum stays, experiences, seasonal rates, cancellation terms, upsells and local positioning. Monitoring those changes can expose revenue opportunities before they become obvious.

## Weekly competitor revenue checklist

Track 3–5 comparable properties for:

- direct-booking benefits and promo codes;
- room packages, minimum stays and seasonal promotions;
- breakfast, transfer, spa, tour and experience upsells;
- cancellation and deposit terms;
- homepage positioning and primary CTA;
- event, romance, family and remote-work packages;
- new destination content and landing pages;
- review positioning and trust signals.

Use the signals to strengthen your own offer rather than copy competitors.

## Revenue opportunities to prioritize

Focus on direct-booking incentives, profitable bundles, underused upsells, event packages, differentiated experiences and conversion friction between room discovery and booking.
