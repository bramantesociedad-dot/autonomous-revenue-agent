# Hotel Guest Experience ROI Calculator

Use this worksheet to estimate whether a branded on-property mission, scavenger hunt or guest experience can create enough incremental revenue to justify development.

## Inputs

- Monthly occupied rooms: **R**
- Average guests per occupied room: **G**
- Participation rate in the experience: **P**
- Average experience revenue per participant: **E**
- Additional food, beverage, retail or partner spend per participant: **U**
- Gross margin on incremental spend: **M**

Estimated monthly participants:

`R × G × P`

Estimated direct experience revenue:

`R × G × P × E`

Estimated ancillary revenue:

`R × G × P × U`

Estimated incremental gross profit:

`R × G × P × (E + U) × M`

## Example

A property with 1,500 occupied rooms/month, 1.8 guests per room and a 10% participation rate creates about 270 participants/month.

If each participant generates $18 in experience revenue plus $12 in ancillary spend:

`270 × ($18 + $12) = $8,100 incremental monthly revenue`

At a 60% gross margin, that is about **$4,860 incremental monthly gross profit** before other operating costs.

## Where branded missions can monetize

Potential monetization points include paid participation, bundled room packages, restaurant/bar checkpoints, retail redemption, sponsored checkpoints, partner commissions, premium family/couple versions, event activations and repeat-visit campaigns.

The experience should be designed around the actual venue, guest flow and commercial objective rather than copied from a generic scavenger hunt.
