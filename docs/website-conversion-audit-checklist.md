# Website Conversion Audit Checklist

A practical website conversion audit for small businesses, SaaS companies, agencies, consultants, local services and e-commerce sites.

Use this checklist to find the most common places where a website loses qualified buyers before they purchase, book or contact you.

## 1. Offer clarity

- Can a first-time visitor understand what you sell in under 10 seconds?
- Is the primary outcome stated before features?
- Is the target customer obvious?
- Is the main offer specific enough to compare against alternatives?

## 2. Call to action

- Is there one dominant next step above the fold?
- Does the CTA describe an outcome rather than a vague action?
- Is the CTA repeated after major proof or benefit sections?
- Can a mobile visitor act without hunting through menus?

## 3. Pricing and buying friction

- Is the price visible when visibility would accelerate the decision?
- If pricing is custom, is there enough information to qualify the buyer?
- Are forms asking only for information required to move the sale forward?
- Are payment, booking or contact steps obvious?

## 4. Trust

- Are there credible proof points near the purchase decision?
- Are guarantees, delivery expectations and refund/cancellation terms clear where relevant?
- Are business identity and contact details easy to verify?
- Does the page avoid unsupported claims and generic hype?

## 5. Message-to-market fit

- Does the page describe the buyer's actual problem in the language buyers use?
- Does each major section answer a buying objection?
- Are alternatives or competing approaches addressed?
- Is the value proposition meaningfully different from competitors?

## 6. Revenue expansion

- Is there an obvious higher-value package or premium option?
- Is there a recurring service where the underlying problem recurs?
- Can a free tool, checklist or score attract buyers before they are ready to purchase?
- Is there a clear path from low-friction entry offer to higher-ticket service?

## Quick scoring

Score each section from 0 to 5. Maximum score: 30.

- **24-30:** strong commercial foundation; optimize experiments and traffic quality.
- **18-23:** meaningful conversion leakage; prioritize offer, CTA and trust fixes.
- **12-17:** substantial friction; restructure the page around the buying decision.
- **0-11:** the website is probably functioning more like a brochure than a sales asset.
