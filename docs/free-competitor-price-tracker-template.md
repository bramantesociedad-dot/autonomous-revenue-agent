## What to check every week

1. **Price:** list price, bundles, financing, minimum order, subscription or membership changes.
2. **Offer:** new packages, bonuses, guarantees, free consultations, trials or lead magnets.
3. **Positioning:** headline, target customer, promised outcome and differentiators.
4. **Promotion:** seasonal offers, urgency, limited inventory, coupons and events.
5. **Conversion path:** primary CTA, form friction, booking flow and checkout steps.
6. **Content:** new landing pages, comparison pages, guides, case studies and campaigns.
7. **Revenue opportunity:** one concrete change worth testing in your own business.

## Weekly decision rule

Do not copy a competitor blindly. Classify each change as one of four signals:

- **Ignore:** cosmetic or irrelevant to your buyer.
- **Watch:** potentially important but needs another week of evidence.
- **Defend:** threatens your conversion, pricing power or differentiation.
- **Exploit:** exposes a gap you can turn into a better offer, message, package or promotion.

The useful output is not a spreadsheet full of observations. It is a short list of revenue actions with a reason to test them.
