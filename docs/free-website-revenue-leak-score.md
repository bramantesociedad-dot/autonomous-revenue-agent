# Free Website Revenue Leak Score

A practical 10-minute self-audit for founders, local businesses, ecommerce stores and service companies that want to identify obvious conversion and revenue leaks on a public website.

## Score your website from 0 to 20

Give yourself **0, 1 or 2 points** on each item.

1. **Offer clarity** — Can a first-time visitor understand what you sell, for whom, and why it matters within five seconds?
2. **Primary CTA** — Is there one obvious next action above the fold?
3. **Price clarity** — Is pricing, a starting price, or a clear path to pricing visible?
4. **Trust** — Are there specific proof points such as case studies, credentials, guarantees, client logos or verifiable outcomes?
5. **Friction** — Can a buyer complete the next step without unnecessary forms, menus or account creation?
6. **Mobile experience** — Is the main CTA visible and easy to use on a phone?
7. **Buyer objections** — Does the page answer the top reasons a prospect might hesitate?
8. **Specificity** — Does the copy describe concrete outcomes instead of generic claims?
9. **Speed to purchase** — Can an interested visitor buy, book or request a quote in under two minutes?
10. **Follow-up path** — If the visitor is not ready to buy, is there a useful next step that keeps them in your funnel?

## Interpretation

- **17–20:** Strong fundamentals. Focus on testing offers, proof and pricing.
- **13–16:** Several likely conversion leaks. Prioritize CTA, trust and friction.
- **8–12:** The site is probably losing qualified buyers before they understand the offer.
- **0–7:** Rebuild the buying path before spending heavily on traffic.

## Three fixes to test first

1. Rewrite the first screen to state **buyer + outcome + offer + CTA**.
2. Put the strongest proof next to the primary CTA.
3. Remove one unnecessary step between interest and purchase/contact.
