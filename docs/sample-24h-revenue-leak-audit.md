## Business snapshot

**Business:** Local professional-service company  
**Primary goal:** More qualified enquiries from existing website traffic  
**Primary conversion:** Book consultation / request quote

## Executive diagnosis

The site is likely losing revenue in three places:

1. **The offer is not concrete enough above the fold.** Visitors understand the category, but not the exact outcome, ideal customer or reason to act now.
2. **The primary CTA asks for too much commitment too early.** A lower-friction first step would capture more high-intent visitors.
3. **Proof appears too late.** Trust signals should support the decision before the first CTA rather than after several sections.

## Priority 1 — Clarify the offer

### Current pattern

Generic headline such as “We help businesses grow.”

### Recommended replacement structure

**Outcome + audience + speed/scope**

Example:

> Get a prioritized conversion plan for your service website in 24 hours — without buying more traffic.

### Revenue rationale

A visitor should understand in seconds:

- what they get;
- who it is for;
- how quickly they get it;
- what action to take next.

## Priority 2 — Create a two-step CTA ladder

Use two distinct actions:

**Primary CTA:** Buy the audit / book the service  
**Secondary CTA:** Run a free revenue-leak score

This preserves a direct purchase path while capturing visitors who are interested but not ready to pay immediately.

## Priority 3 — Move proof next to the decision

Place relevant proof immediately before or beside the first purchase CTA:

- exact deliverables;
- turnaround time;
- who the service is for;
- example findings;
- refund/cancellation terms if applicable;
- a sample deliverable.

Avoid invented testimonials or unsupported performance claims.

## Priority 4 — Reduce checkout ambiguity

Before the Stripe button, state exactly what happens after payment:

1. Buyer submits website and commercial goal.
2. Site is reviewed for offer, CTA, trust, pricing/packaging and conversion friction.
3. Buyer receives a prioritized action plan.

The purchase should feel like buying a defined deliverable, not an open-ended consulting engagement.

## 7-day implementation order

**Day 1:** Rewrite headline and first CTA.  
**Day 2:** Move proof above the first major scroll break.  
**Day 3:** Add lower-friction secondary CTA.  
**Day 4:** Clarify deliverables directly above checkout.  
**Day 5:** Remove unnecessary form fields or duplicate decision points.  
**Day 6:** Compare competitor offer framing and pricing presentation.  
**Day 7:** Review conversion events and decide the next test.
